### Hi, I'm Shreyas 👋

For 17 years I taught JEE Physics to lakhs of students at Vedantu, Unacademy, and Physics Wallah. Most recently I was Head of Department at Vedantu, where I owned hiring, training, and curriculum for a team teaching hundreds of thousands of students. B.Tech Mechanical from VNIT Nagpur, AIR 3920 in JEE 2005, and a few years as an engineer at National Instruments before coaching took over.

I'm now moving into product and tech. The projects below are how I've been learning to build, not just teach: full apps, an autonomous trading agent, and a game engine, all built solo.

---

#### 🧭 [CapCompass](https://capcompass.app): an AI study planner for JEE/NEET students

A single-file, offline-first PWA that plans a student's entire year: roadmap, weekly timetable, revision tracking, test scheduling with score projection, and an AI study copilot, all running on-device with optional Google Drive sync. Built solo end to end: product design, UX, and the engineering to ship it as an installable app with no backend.

What it demonstrates: scoping a real product from a real classroom problem (I've watched this exact planning problem fail for thousands of students), and shipping it as a live, working product rather than a mockup.

---

#### 📈 Nifty Scalp Sniper: an autonomous options trading agent

A self-observing Nifty options agent that moves through disciplined phases: observe signals with no money at risk, paper-trade with full P&L tracking and daily AI-written journals, validate the strategy against real criteria, and only then go live, with hard safety limits (position caps, daily loss limits, time-of-day restrictions, a kill switch). Two years of backtesting sit behind the current signal logic.

What it demonstrates: quantitative thinking, risk-first system design, and comfort working through ambiguity with real capital on the line eventually. Source is private (trading logic is the whole edge), happy to walk through the architecture.

---

#### 🚂 TrainYard: a toy train builder game

A browser-based 3D train yard builder: procedural track laying, signalling, and train AI, built on Three.js with a glTF pipeline so 3D assets can be modelled in Blender and dropped straight in. Started as a single HTML file, since rebuilt on Vite with hot-reload for faster iteration.

What it demonstrates: I can pick up a graphics/simulation stack I'd never touched before and get something playable working.

---

#### 📡 [Maha-Edu Monitor](https://github.com/Shreyashebbare/maha-edu-monitor): automated board/CET news alerts

A zero-server scraper that checks Maharashtra's HSC board, MHT-CET, and admissions portals every 30 minutes and pushes new notices straight to a phone, running free on GitHub Actions with no server and no hosting bill.

What it demonstrates: shipping automation that runs unattended and stays reliable (dedup, self-healing scheduling), not just a one-off script.
